Privacy policy for Tossakan Thailand Watch Face

Effective 5 November 2026. Unlimited Dreams Eleven publishes the Wear OS watch face and the optional Android companion app under the package ID com.ud11.tossakan.watchface.

The watch face displays the time, date, battery level and step count provided by Wear OS on your watch. These values are used on the device to render the watch face. The watch face does not send them to Unlimited Dreams Eleven or to third parties.

The companion app shows example watch faces and setup instructions. It does not require an account or include advertising or analytics. Its Google Play button opens an external Google service, which is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Neither component collects, transmits, sells or shares personal data. There is no developer-held account or user-data record to delete. You can remove either component from its device using the device's standard uninstall controls.

Questions about this policy can be sent to ud11.support@gmail.com.
