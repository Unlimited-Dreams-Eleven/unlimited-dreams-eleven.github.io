Privacy policy for Tossakan Thailand Watch Face

Updated 17 November 2026. Unlimited Dreams Eleven publishes the Wear OS watch face and the optional Android companion app under the package ID com.ud11.tossakan.watchface.

The watch face displays the time, date, battery level and step count provided by Wear OS on your watch. These values are used on the device to render the watch face. The watch face does not send them to Unlimited Dreams Eleven or to third parties.

The companion app shows example watch faces and setup instructions. It does not require an account or include advertising or analytics. Its Google Play button opens an external Google service, which is governed by Google's privacy policy.

The companion uses Google Play services to find connected watches and show their display names locally. Tapping Install to wearable sends a request through Google Play services to open the Google Play listing on the selected watch. Connection information is not sent to the developer. You confirm installation or any purchase in Google Play. The feedback button opens your email app, where you choose what to send. If you email support, the developer receives your message and reply address to handle your request. You can request deletion of that correspondence by email.

Neither component automatically collects, transmits, sells or shares personal data. The app does not create a developer-held user account. You can remove either component from its device using the device's standard uninstall controls.

Questions about this policy can be sent to ud11.support@gmail.com.
