Eternal Embrace for Garmin — Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026 · Unlimited Dreams Eleven · Garmin Connect IQ edition

This notice explains how Eternal Embrace for Garmin handles watch data, Gumroad licensing, and support requests.

Data displayed on the watch

The face uses the watch clock, device settings and available Garmin services to display time, date, battery, steps, heart rate and weather. Garmin may supply recent heart-rate readings through its sensor history API. These readings are used for display on the watch and are not included in license requests. You can hide the data fields in the face’s settings.

Purchase and license verification

Gumroad processes purchases and sends your receipt. When you submit a license key, the watch stores it locally and sends it with the product identifiers and an app-specific watch identifier over HTTPS to our license service. The service asks Gumroad to verify the purchase, including whether it is a test, refund or disputed order. We use the result to activate the key on one watch, check continued access and handle replacement requests. The watch retains the time of the last successful check to allow temporary offline use.

Our Cloudflare Workers and D1 service keeps hashed versions of license, sale and watch identifiers, verification timestamps and binding revisions. A support transfer records the previous and new bindings, a case reference, reason and revision. The binding database does not store card details, buyer email addresses or raw keys or watch identifiers. Cloudflare may process connection metadata, such as IP addresses and request timing, for delivery, security and operational logs. License keys are sent in the request body. Our application does not log them.

Service providers

Gumroad handles checkout, payment details and receipts. Garmin handles Connect IQ delivery and watch services. These providers process information under their own policies. Gumroad privacy policy · Garmin privacy information · Cloudflare privacy policy

Support, retention and your choices

License records are kept as needed to provide access, enforce one-watch licensing, handle transfers and resolve disputes. If you contact support, we use the information you provide to verify and resolve your request. For a replacement watch, we may ask for your order reference and the new watch’s Support ID. Please do not send payment-card details or health readings.

To request access, correction or deletion, or to ask about this notice, email ud11.support@gmail.com. We may need to verify the request or retain records required for licensing, dispute resolution or applicable obligations. Uninstalling the watch face removes its local settings but does not itself erase the license binding on the service.
