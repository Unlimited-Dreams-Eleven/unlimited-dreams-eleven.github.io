Eternal Embrace — Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 November 2026 · Developer: Unlimited Dreams Eleven
Wear OS application: com.ud11.eternalembrace

This policy covers the Eternal Embrace watch face for Wear OS and its optional Android phone installation companion, distributed under com.ud11.eternalembrace through Google Play. It does not cover a separate Garmin product or future payment services for other platforms.

Information handled by the watch face

Eternal Embrace is a resource-only Watch Face Format app. It contains artwork and watch-face descriptions rendered by Wear OS. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK, developer-operated backend, or network code. We do not collect, sell, or transmit your personal information through this Wear OS app.

The face displays time, date, day/night artwork, and information in complication slots. Wear OS and the complication providers you select supply and render that information, such as battery level or step count. A selected provider may display fitness information, including heart rate, where supported. Eternal Embrace does not measure those values, request sensor or Health Connect permissions, store a health history, or send complication values to us. Availability, permission controls, and data practices of a provider are governed by that provider and Wear OS.

Choices and local settings

You can change or hide complications in the watch-face editor and manage provider permissions in your watch settings. Local watch-face preferences are managed by Wear OS. You can remove the face by uninstalling it. No developer-held in-app account or personal-data database exists to delete. Because this is a watch face, displayed fitness values are for general information, not diagnosis, treatment, or medical decisions.

Phone installation companion

The optional phone app displays bundled static previews and installation instructions. It does not read watch complications, pair with a watch, measure sensors, or collect or transmit personal information. It has no app permissions, advertising, analytics SDK or network client. Your selected preview is held locally while the app is open. Buttons open Google Play or this privacy page in an external app. The support button opens your email app with our address and a subject; no message is sent automatically. Those external services handle data under their own policies. The animated watch face runs separately on your watch.

Google Play and other services

Google Play may process your account, purchase, installation, and optional diagnostic information under Google's own policies. We do not receive your payment-card details from the watch face. Provider apps and the operating system may independently process information under their own privacy policies.

Support messages

If you email us voluntarily, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and resolve your support request, retain it only as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations, and do not sell it. Please avoid sending sensitive health information. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to obligations that require retention.

Age group

The intended audience is users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this watch face.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date if the app's data practices change. Contact Unlimited Dreams Eleven at ud11.support@gmail.com for privacy or support questions.
