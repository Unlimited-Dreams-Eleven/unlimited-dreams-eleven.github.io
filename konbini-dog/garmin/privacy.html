Garmin Connect IQ Privacy Policy

Developer: Unlimited Dreams Eleven
Contact: ud11.support@gmail.com
Updated: 28 October 2026

This policy applies to the Garmin Connect IQ edition. The Wear OS edition has a separate policy.

Information displayed on your watch

The watch face reads time, date, battery level, step count, cached Garmin weather and the latest available heart-rate history sample to display the information you select. Heart-rate history uses the SensorHistory permission. These values are used on your device and are not sent to the developer or included in KiezelPay licensing requests. Unavailable readings are shown without a value. Weather comes from Garmin's cached weather service; the watch face does not directly request your location.

Payments and licensing

This edition uses KiezelPay for a 24-hour trial and a one-time US$2.99 unlock. Taxes or currency conversion may affect checkout totals. The Background and Communications permissions allow the licensing service to communicate through your connected phone and internet connection. KiezelPay processes the Garmin installation identifier, product identity and trial/license status to validate access. Purchase and support information provided during checkout is processed by KiezelPay and its payment providers under their own policies. Card details and payment passwords are not entered on the watch or collected by the developer.

See the KiezelPay Privacy Policy and Garmin Connect IQ Privacy Policy.

Local settings and other services

Season, lighting, temperature units, motion and five data choices are stored in Connect IQ application settings. The watch face contains no advertising, analytics, developer account sign-in or developer-operated data backend.

Support and your choices

You can change the displayed data and settings, manage device permissions or uninstall the watch face. Emails you choose to send to ud11.support@gmail.com are used to answer your request. Please do not send payment credentials or unnecessary health data. For questions about information processed by KiezelPay or Garmin, contact the relevant provider.
