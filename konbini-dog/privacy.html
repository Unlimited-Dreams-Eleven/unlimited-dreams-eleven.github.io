Konbini Dog Seasons

Wear OS and Android Companion Privacy Policy

Developer: Unlimited Dreams Eleven
Contact: ud11.support@gmail.com
Updated: 27 October 2026

Information displayed on your device

The watch face displays time, date, battery, weather and information supplied by the complication providers you select. Providers may display activity or health information, such as steps and heart rate, subject to their permissions and availability. Konbini Dog Seasons does not request location directly or operate a weather server.

Collection and sharing

The Wear OS watch face and Android companion contain no advertising, analytics, account registration, or developer-operated network service. They do not collect or transmit your watch data, health values, location, or weather information to the developer or a developer server. The companion displays bundled sample artwork and installation guidance. It does not read watch health data or synchronize watch settings.

Settings and retention

Your preferences are managed locally by the watch and Android platform. The developer has no server-side user profile to retain or delete. Uninstalling removes app-managed local data according to platform behavior.

Google Play and other services

Google Play handles store purchases and account information under its own policies. System services and third-party complication providers operate under their own privacy policies. The watch face does not process purchase credentials. Any Garmin edition is a separate product and purchase.

Support correspondence

If you choose to contact support, your email provider sends your message and the information you include. We use that correspondence to answer your request. Do not include payment credentials or sensitive health information.

Your choices and policy changes

You may change or hide complication slots, manage provider permissions, or uninstall the app through your device. Contact us with privacy questions. We will update this policy when the app's data practices change.
