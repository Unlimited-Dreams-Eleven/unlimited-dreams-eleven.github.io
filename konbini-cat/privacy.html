Wear OS and Android companion Privacy Policy

Effective: 27 October 2026
Developer: Unlimited Dreams Eleven
Contact: ud11.support@gmail.com

Information used on your device

The watch face displays time, date, system weather and information from the complication providers you select. The watch face can also display battery, step-count and heart-rate values supplied directly by Wear OS. Selected complication providers may supply additional activity or health information according to their permissions and availability. The watch face does not request your location directly and does not operate a weather server.

Collection and sharing

The watch face and Android companion contain no advertising, analytics SDK, developer account registration or developer-operated backend. They do not send health, activity, location or weather information to the developer. The companion displays bundled sample images and setup instructions; it does not read watch health data or synchronize watch settings.

Settings and retention

Watch-face settings are managed locally by the watch and platform. The developer does not keep a server-side profile of your watch data. You can change or hide data positions, manage provider permissions and uninstall the app using your device. Removal of local data follows platform behavior.

Purchases and other services

Google Play handles purchases and account information under its own policies. The watch face does not process payment credentials. System weather and third-party complication providers are subject to their own privacy policies. Garmin purchases are separate from the Wear OS edition.

Support

If you email support, your email provider sends your message and the information you choose to include. We use support correspondence to respond to your request. Please do not include payment credentials or unnecessary health information.

Updates and questions

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. Contact ud11.support@gmail.com with privacy questions.
