Garmin Connect IQ Privacy Policy

Effective: 27 October 2026
Developer: Unlimited Dreams Eleven
Contact: ud11.support@gmail.com

Information used on your watch

The watch face reads time, date, battery level, step count, Garmin's cached weather and the latest available heart-rate history sample to display information you select. Heart-rate history access uses the SensorHistory permission. Unavailable values are left empty. The app uses Garmin weather data rather than requesting your location directly.

Collection and sharing

The app contains no advertising, analytics SDK, developer account registration or developer-operated backend. It does not transmit health, activity, location or weather information to the developer. KiezelPay performs purchase and trial licensing as described below.

Local preferences

Season, lighting, temperature units, motion and five data preferences are saved using Connect IQ application settings. The developer has no server-side health profile. You can hide data, change settings, manage permissions or uninstall using your watch and Garmin apps.

Payments and licensing

KiezelPay handles trial and purchase licensing for this edition. Licensing requests use the Garmin installation identifier, this app's product identifier and trial or license status through the connected phone and internet. The app's Background and Communications permissions support licensing. Payment credentials are not entered on the watch or processed by the developer's watch-face code.

KiezelPay and its checkout providers process purchase information under their own policies. See the KiezelPay Privacy Policy. Garmin services are subject to Garmin's policies. A purchase of the Garmin edition does not unlock the separate Wear OS edition.

Support

If you email support, your email provider sends your message and the information you choose to include. We use support correspondence to respond to your request. Please do not include payment credentials or unnecessary health information.

Updates and questions

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. Contact ud11.support@gmail.com with privacy questions.
