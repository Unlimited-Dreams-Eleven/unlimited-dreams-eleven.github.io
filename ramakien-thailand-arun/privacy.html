This policy describes the Wear OS watch face and optional Android phone installation guide distributed through Google Play. It does not describe the separate Garmin edition.

This watch face is a resource-only Watch Face Format package. It contains no app analytics, advertising SDK, account system, custom network client or developer-operated backend. The developer does not collect or transmit personal data through the watch face.

The phone companion displays bundled previews and a setup guide. It uses Google Play services Wearable APIs to discover connected Wear OS watches, reading their connection identifiers and display names temporarily in memory. When you tap Install on watch and choose a watch, the AndroidX RemoteActivityHelper asks that watch to open this product in Google Play. The app does not silently install software, confirm installation, change your watch face, or scan Bluetooth directly. Watch names and connection identifiers are not saved, logged, or sent to a developer server. Google Play services and the watch companion system handle device communication under their own privacy policies.

The phone companion does not access weather, location, health data or complication values and has no analytics, advertising, account system, share tracking or developer-operated backend. Rating, Google Play, privacy, support and share actions open external apps only when you choose them. Sharing uses the Android share sheet with the public product link; you choose the recipient and confirm sending. The developer does not receive a record of these actions. First-party app screens do not request Bluetooth, location or health permissions. A local preference records whether the setup invitation has been shown; it stays on the phone and is not used for tracking.

Time, weekday/month labels, weather/temperature and selected complication values are rendered by Wear OS and its configured providers. Weather may use location handled by the system weather service. This app does not request location permission, obtain a precise address, or send location to the developer. Complication values such as battery or steps are displayed locally; their original providers have their own privacy practices.

Google Play handles payment and app delivery under Google's policies. There is no external payment flow or license-key collection in the Wear OS package.

If you contact support, your email address and the information you voluntarily provide are used to respond to your request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or unnecessary health or location information. Contact the support address for questions about information sent to support.

This policy may be updated to reflect product changes. Any future data collection would require an updated disclosure.
