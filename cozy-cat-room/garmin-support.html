Cozy Cat Room for Garmin

A peaceful cat companion for compatible round AMOLED Garmin watches.

Trial and purchase

The Garmin edition includes a 24-hour free trial, followed by a US$2.99 one-time KiezelPay unlock. It is a separate purchase from the Wear OS edition. After the trial ends, the watch displays a code. Visit www.kzl.io/code, enter the code and check that the product is Cozy Cat Room before proceeding. KiezelPay shows the checkout total before payment. Keep your receipt.

Already purchased?

Enter the current watch code at the same KiezelPay page and choose Already Purchased. Follow KiezelPay's restoration steps. Your watch and connected phone need an internet connection for license validation; it can take a few minutes to refresh. Contact support if you cannot restore your purchase. Do not share payment-card details or a license code publicly.

Weather and day/night

Automatic weather depends on Garmin's weather synchronization. Missing readings show -- and older temperature data is marked with an asterisk. Day/night uses sunrise and sunset from recent weather when available; otherwise, it follows a local 06:00–18:59 daytime approximation. Check the watch's time zone and Garmin phone synchronization. Room lighting and Weather preview settings can be set back to Auto after trying a manual scene.

Motion and always-on mode

Cat and precipitation motion runs only during the active watch-face interval allowed by Garmin. It stops in low power. Always-on artwork is deliberately sparse to meet display protection requirements. Brightness, visibility, animation and battery usage vary by watch and settings.
