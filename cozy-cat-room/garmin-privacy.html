Cozy Cat Room for Garmin

This privacy notice applies to the Garmin Connect IQ edition of Cozy Cat Room, published by Unlimited Dreams Eleven.

Information displayed on your watch

The watch face reads the clock, date, battery, activity information, optional recent heart rate, notification count and Garmin weather to display your selected details. It does not read notification contents. The display uses these readings on the watch. We do not operate health-data uploads, advertising or analytics for this edition.

Weather and daylight

Weather comes from Garmin. When available, the watch face uses the observation location supplied with recent Garmin weather to determine daylight. The watch face does not activate GPS or send the observation location to our licensing service. Garmin and your connected phone manage their own synchronization and data processing.

Trial, purchases and license verification

Version 1.2.0 and later store the trial and license state on the watch. To verify a Gumroad license, the watch sends the product ID, app ID, license key and Garmin watch identifier over HTTPS to our Cloudflare-hosted license service. The service checks the license with Gumroad and records scoped hashes of the key, sale and watch identifier, the product and environment, verification times, and transfer audit records. Our licensing code does not store card data or buyer emails in the binding database. Network providers may process network metadata to deliver and protect the service.

Gumroad and its payment providers handle checkout and the data entered there. See the Gumroad privacy policy. Version 1.0.0 used KiezelPay for checkout and licensing under its privacy policy. Version 1.2.0 can read the verified legacy purchase marker on the watch to carry forward eligible Cozy purchases.

Support and retention

Email ud11.support@gmail.com for help or for privacy requests about records we hold. For a watch replacement we may request your purchase reference and the new Watch support ID to verify the order and transfer the binding. We retain license and support records as needed to provide the purchase, resolve issues and meet applicable obligations. Contact the relevant payment provider about its checkout records.

Platform scope

The Garmin and Wear OS editions are separate. Garmin, Connect IQ, your phone and payment providers have their own privacy policies.
