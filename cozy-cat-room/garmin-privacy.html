Cozy Cat Room for Garmin

Privacy Policy — effective 29 October 2026. This policy applies to the Garmin Connect IQ edition published by UnlimitedDreamsEleven.

Information displayed on your watch

The watch face reads the clock, date, battery, activity information, optional recent heart rate, notification count and Garmin weather to render your selected details. Notification contents are not read. Our display code uses these readings on the watch; we do not operate a health-data upload service or an advertising or analytics service. Settings are stored in Connect IQ application storage.

Weather and daylight

Weather comes from Garmin's native weather service. Sunrise and sunset use the observation location supplied with recent Garmin weather when available. The watch face does not activate GPS or operate a separate weather server. If recent solar information is unavailable, it uses a local-clock approximation. Garmin and your connected phone control their own synchronization and data processing.

Trial, purchase and license validation

This edition includes KiezelPay for a 24-hour trial and a one-time US$2.99 unlock. The KiezelPay library communicates with its service to manage the trial and verify a purchase or license. The watch therefore requests Communications and Background permissions, in addition to SensorHistory for optional recent heart rate. Licensing is separate from the health and activity display code.

Purchase and license processing can involve product, device or purchase identifiers, license status and network metadata. Information you enter at the hosted checkout is handled by KiezelPay and its payment providers under their own policies. The developer may have access to purchase or license records needed to supply the unlock and handle support. Payment-card entry is not part of the watch face. See KiezelPay's Privacy Policy for its collection and processing practices. We do not claim that this Garmin edition sends no data off the device.

Support and retention

Contact ud11.support@gmail.com for support or requests concerning records held by the developer. We use information you choose to send to address your request. Purchase, license and support records are retained as needed to provide the purchased service, resolve issues and meet applicable obligations. Contact KiezelPay for requests concerning its checkout or payment records. Please avoid sending health readings or precise location unless you choose to and they are necessary to explain your issue.

Platform scope and changes

A Wear OS or Google Play purchase does not grant a Garmin license. Garmin, Connect IQ, the connected phone and payment providers have their own privacy policies. This page will be updated if this edition's data practices change.
