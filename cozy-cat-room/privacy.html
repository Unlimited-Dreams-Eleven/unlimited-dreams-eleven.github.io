Cozy Cat Room — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

Cozy Cat Room is published by Unlimited Dreams Eleven. This policy covers the Wear OS watch face and its Android phone preview and installation companion, package com.ud11.cozycatroom.watchface.

Watch face data

The watch face uses the Wear OS system renderer and system data sources to display time, date, weather, temperature and user-selected complications such as battery, steps or heart rate. These values are displayed locally. The watch face does not contain its own analytics, advertising, account system, direct sensor collection, GPS tracking service or developer-operated weather server.

Weather and daylight information are supplied by the device's weather service. That service may use location and network access under its own settings and privacy policy. The watch face does not independently request precise location or send location or health readings to Unlimited Dreams Eleven. Data availability, permissions, accuracy and update schedules depend on the operating system and selected providers. Outdated or unavailable daylight data may cause the face to use an approximate local-clock fallback.

Complications and settings

The operating system manages complication provider selection and watch-face settings. Providers may have their own privacy policies. Selecting an icon changes its appearance only and does not grant access to a different data source. No readings are uploaded to the developer by this watch face.

Phone companion

The phone app shows bundled offline sample images and installation guidance. It does not read watch health information, retrieve live weather, track location, require an account, or include advertising or analytics. When you choose an external Google Play or privacy-policy link, your browser or Google Play opens that destination and its policies apply.

Google Play and support

Google Play manages distribution, testing access and any store purchase or billing records under Google's policies. We do not receive your payment-card details through this app. If you contact support by email, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to answer your request. Please do not send passwords, payment-card information or unnecessary health or location data. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and related recordkeeping; you may request deletion by contacting us, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information or operate a backend that stores watch-face readings. On-device settings and provider data are managed by your operating system. This policy does not replace the privacy policies of Google, your device manufacturer, your weather service or your selected complication providers.

Children

The product does not provide an account, chat or a developer-operated personal-data collection feature for children. Device accounts, store access and parental controls are managed by the relevant platform.

This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting and linked services may process ordinary request information under their own policies. See GitHub's privacy statement and Google's privacy policy.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app's behavior changes. The revised policy will show its effective date here.
