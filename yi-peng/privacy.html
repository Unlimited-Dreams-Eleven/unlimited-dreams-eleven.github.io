Privacy policy

Effective 2 November 2026

The phone companion provides offline previews and installation guidance. It has no account, advertisements, analytics SDK, network permission, direct health access or location tracking. Preview values are examples and are not measurements from your watch.

The Wear OS watch face displays time, date, and data supplied by the watch system or the complication providers you select. Battery, steps, heart rate and weather values are displayed within the device/platform. The watch face has no application code, server, account or analytics SDK that sends those values to Unlimited Dreams Eleven.

Health and weather services and other complication providers manage their own permissions, availability, data processing and privacy policies. Weather may rely on a location known to the platform or its provider. We do not receive that location or the displayed readings.

The Google Play and privacy buttons open external apps or websites governed by their own policies. Google Play handles purchases and payment information. We do not receive your payment card details.

If you choose to email support, we receive the email address and content you send and use them to respond. Contact us if you wish to request deletion of your correspondence.

You can uninstall the apps to remove their local app data and manage complication providers in your watch settings. The apps do not require an account, so there is no in-app account to delete.
