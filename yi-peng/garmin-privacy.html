Privacy policy from Unlimited Dreams Eleven. Effective 10 November 2026.

Data displayed on your watch

The watch face uses your settings, battery level, activity summaries, recent heart rate and cached Garmin weather to display information. It does not send these readings to the developer, the license service or Gumroad.

Trial and license verification

The watch stores the trial start and verified license locally. To activate or recheck a purchased license, the watch sends the license key, Gumroad product ID, Garmin app ID and app-specific watch identifier to the developer’s Cloudflare license service over HTTPS. The service sends the key and product ID to Gumroad for verification. The watch identifier is used by the license service to bind the license to one watch.

The service stores scoped SHA-256 digests of the key, sale reference and watch identifier, together with the product, test or live environment, verification timestamps and binding revision. Support transfers record the previous and new watch digests, the revision, time, reason and support case reference. The licensing database does not store raw keys, raw watch identifiers, buyer emails or card details. Cloudflare may process ordinary request information for hosting and security.

Purchases, support and deletion

Gumroad handles checkout and receipts under its own privacy policy. If you email support, we use the information you send to resolve your request. For a replacement watch, provide your order number and Watch Support ID. Keep passwords, full card details and health information out of support requests.

Email ud11.support@gmail.com to ask about your licensing data or request access or deletion. Removing a binding may affect access to the paid watch face.

See the Gumroad privacy policy, Cloudflare privacy policy and GitHub Privacy Statement for details about checkout, licensing infrastructure and policy page hosting.
