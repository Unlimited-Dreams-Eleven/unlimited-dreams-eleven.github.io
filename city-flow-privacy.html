Privacy policy

Developer: Unlimited Dreams Eleven
Contact: ud11.support@gmail.com
Updated: 21 November 2026 (optional purchases, revenue measurement and retention)

The game can be played without an account. Game progress, lives, language and audio preferences are stored locally on your device. Optional cloud saves use Google Play Games Services; we do not operate a separate player-data server.

Advertising

The Android app integrates Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. AdMob Mediation may also select ads from AppLovin and Unity Ads when those partners are enabled. These partners may process advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, device/app information, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention, according to your privacy choices. See AppLovin's privacy policy and Unity's game player privacy policy. We forward supported consent and opt-out signals to these partners; permission for optional gameplay analytics does not authorize personalized advertising. Depending on your region and consent choices, Google and its partners may process device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud and meet legal requirements. See Google's privacy policy and advertising information. Development and test builds use Google's sample ad units. Advertising requests require internet access.

Rewarded ads are optional. A heart is awarded only when the advertising SDK confirms the reward. Ad availability depends on the network and consent state. Banner ads appear separately from the play area. Interstitial ads appear only between levels.

Optional purchases

A one-time, non-consumable Google Play purchase removes banner and between-level advertisements. Optional rewarded ads remain available. Google Play handles payments and keeps the purchase associated with your Google Play account. We do not receive your card or bank details. The app verifies and stores a signed purchase receipt locally to restore access offline and checks purchase ownership with Google Play when online. Refunds and revocations are reflected after a successful ownership check. Use Restore purchases with the same Google Play account after reinstalling or changing devices. Purchase receipts and tokens are not sent to Analytics or Saved Games.

Optional gameplay analytics

With your permission, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure sessions, level starts and results, tutorial usage, app engagement, returning play days, checkout activity, purchases and advertising revenue. Standard Firebase purchase and AdMob revenue events provide monetization reports; diagnostic paid-ad callbacks are kept separate to avoid double counting. Returning-player measurement begins only after analytics is enabled. Google may process app-instance/device identifiers, device and app information, IP-derived approximate region, and interactions. Analytics is off until you choose to enable it. We do not send your email, Play Games player ID, profile name, precise location or gameplay input recordings to Analytics. Analytics consent does not grant permission for personalised advertising. You can turn analytics off in Settings → Cloud save & analytics; the app then stops collection and resets its local Analytics identity. This does not automatically erase data already processed by Google. See Firebase privacy information and contact us for privacy requests.

Optional cloud progress

Google Play Games may automatically authenticate an existing Play Games profile when the Android app starts. Users without a profile can continue playing without creating one. Uploading progress remains optional and starts only after you choose Connect Play Games.

When you choose Connect Play Games, Google Play Games Services uses your Google/Play Games identity to store your best stars, unlocked-level progress and tutorial completion in a private Saved Games slot associated with your Play Games account. Google operates authentication and cloud storage. We do not receive your password, email or authentication tokens. A hashed player identifier is kept locally solely to prevent progress from different accounts being mixed; it is not sent to Analytics. Hearts, regeneration timers, language and audio settings remain on the device. Saves may queue offline and upload when the device reconnects. Turning off cloud saving stops future app sync requests but does not delete existing cloud saves. You can manage or delete your game data in Google Play Games settings; uninstalling the game alone does not delete cloud data.

Your choices

You can access privacy choices from the game menu. Where Google requires it, the app provides a consent form and a privacy-options form. Device settings may also let you manage or reset your advertising identifier. Uninstalling the app removes its local game data.

Contact and changes

The game does not ask for exact birth dates or precise location. If you contact us by email, we use the information you provide to respond to your request. Contact the developer with questions or privacy requests. We will update this policy if data handling changes.
