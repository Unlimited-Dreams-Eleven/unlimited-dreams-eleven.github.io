Privacy policy

Developer: Unlimited Dreams Eleven
Contact: ud11.support@gmail.com
Effective: 29 October 2026

Game progress, lives, language and audio preferences are stored locally on your device. The game does not require an account or operate a player-data server.

Advertising

The Android app integrates Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Depending on your region and consent choices, Google and its partners may process device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud and meet legal requirements. See Google's privacy policy and advertising information. Development and test builds use Google's sample ad units. Advertising requests require internet access.

Rewarded ads are optional. A heart is awarded only when the advertising SDK confirms the reward. Ad availability depends on the network and consent state. Banner ads appear separately from the play area. Interstitial ads appear only between levels.

Your choices

You can access privacy choices from the game menu. Where Google requires it, the app provides a consent form and a privacy-options form. Device settings may also let you manage or reset your advertising identifier. Uninstalling the app removes its local game data.

Contact and changes

The game does not ask for player profiles, exact birth dates or precise location. If you contact us by email, we use the information you provide to respond to your request. Contact the developer with questions or privacy requests. We will update this policy if data handling changes.
