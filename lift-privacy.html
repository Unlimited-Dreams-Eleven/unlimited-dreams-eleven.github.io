Privacy Policy

LIFT City Stories is provided by Unlimited Dreams Eleven. Updated: 2 November 2026.

Game progress

Stars, unlocked levels, the last selected level, hearts, heart-recovery timestamps and tutorial acknowledgement are stored locally on your device. Heart-recovery timestamps let the game restore hearts while it is closed. There is no player account, Google sign-in requirement or developer-operated player-data server. The current Android app disables Android app-data backup. Your desktop-browser progress does not automatically transfer to the Android app.

Advertising

The Android app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google and its partners may process IP addresses, which can indicate approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing. Debug builds use test ads; release builds use the app's registered AdMob units. Advertising needs an internet connection; local gameplay does not.

Banner ads occupy a separate area below the game. Interstitial ads appear between completed levels. Rewarded ads are optional: you may choose to watch one for a heart or a hint. Rewards are granted only when the advertising SDK confirms completion. Closing an ad early does not grant a reward.

Data security

Game progress stays in local app storage and is not sent to a game server. It is not separately encrypted by the game; access also depends on device security. The Android app blocks mixed-content loading and direct file/content access in its WebView. Google handles advertising data under its own security practices. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Please keep your device and app updated.

Your choices

Where required, Google UMP asks for advertising privacy choices before ads load. The app's hearts dialog includes an Ad privacy choices entry for Google's privacy-options form. Availability depends on Google's consent requirements. You can also manage, reset or delete your advertising identifier through supported Android settings. Watching rewarded ads is optional, and hearts also recover with time.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you clear app storage or uninstall the app. Clearing storage removes saved stars, unlocks and hearts. In a browser, clearing this game's site data removes its local records. Google controls retention of data handled by its advertising services; see its privacy policy. We do not sell player data or receive individual advertising identifiers through a game backend.

Permissions and personal information

The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location, and does not ask for your date of birth. Google advertising services may still infer approximate location from an IP address. If you contact support by email, we use the information you provide to answer your request. This information is handled through the email provider, not stored as a player profile inside the game.

Children's privacy

We do not intentionally request personal profiles or identifying details from children through the game. Advertising data is handled by Google's SDK as described above. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact support. This policy does not represent an independent age-verification system.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the game's data handling changes. The date above identifies the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Unlimited Dreams Eleven at ud11.support@gmail.com. Mention LIFT City Stories and describe your request; please do not send passwords or advertising identifiers.
