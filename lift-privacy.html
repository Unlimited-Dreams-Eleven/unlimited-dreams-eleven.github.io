Privacy Policy

LIFT: Elevator Puzzle (LIFT City Stories) is provided by Unlimited Dreams Eleven. Updated: 24 November 2026. Android version 0.3.2 includes optional Analytics, cloud saves and a connected shop. Shop availability depends on the configured release and account.

Game progress and optional cloud saves

Stars, unlocked levels, the last selected level, hearts, recovery-clock references, free tools, weekly mission progress and tutorial acknowledgement are stored locally on your device. Local gameplay does not require signing in. Android app-data backup remains disabled.

From version 0.3.0, you may choose Connect & merge progress in Cloud save & privacy to use Google Play Games Saved Games. Your earned stars are saved in a private game save associated with your Google Play Games account. Across devices, the game keeps the highest star result for each level and derives unlocked levels and total stars from those results. Repeated syncs do not add duplicate stars. Hearts, timers, language, sound settings and Analytics consent are not uploaded in this game save. Sync requires a supported Google Play Games account and connectivity; Google may cache and upload saves when the device reconnects.

The app keeps a one-way hash of your Play Games player identifier locally to avoid mixing saves between accounts. It is not sent to Firebase Analytics. Optional shop authentication and purchase verification are described below. We do not receive your Google password or payment-card details. Cloud sync can be turned off in the game menu; this stops future sync requests and does not delete an existing Google cloud save. Desktop-browser progress does not automatically transfer to Android.

Reward timing

Version 0.3.1 uses Android's elapsed-time counter and a network-time reference for heart recovery and weekly missions. Changing the device date or time does not speed up rewards. When Android cannot supply network time, the app may make a background HTTPS request for the date header of this public privacy page. No player identifier, advertising identifier, account information or game progress is included in that request. The website host receives normal connection information, including the IP address, under its own privacy practices. This timing check is independent of optional Analytics. When time cannot be verified after a reboot, the app continues counting known elapsed time and waits for a valid reference before crediting unknown powered-off time.

Optional Firebase Analytics

From version 0.3.0, gameplay Analytics is off by default and Analytics collection is enabled only after you choose to enable Share gameplay analytics. Connecting the optional shop may initialize Firebase Authentication separately while Analytics collection stays off. You can change this choice at any time in Cloud save & privacy. The setting is saved on that device and is independent of Google Play Games and advertising privacy choices. Declining Analytics does not affect gameplay, stars, hearts or cloud saves.

If enabled, Firebase Analytics processes events such as level starts and results, stops used, stars earned, decision time, and tutorial completion or skipping, together with app-instance, device and usage information handled by the SDK. These data help us understand difficulty and improve the game. The game does not send your Play Games player identifier, Google email, cloud save or free-text messages as Analytics event parameters. Analytics Advertising ID collection and advertising-personalization signals are disabled. Gameplay events that occur before opt-in are not queued for later upload.

Turning Analytics off disables collection, withdraws Analytics storage consent and resets Analytics data held locally by the SDK. It does not automatically remove data already processed by Google. Contact support for questions about data already collected; Google also provides account and privacy controls.

Advertising

The Android app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google and its partners may process IP addresses, which can indicate approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing. Current Internal and Closed test builds use Google's demo ad inventory for every ad format. A future production build may use registered AdMob units. Advertising needs an internet connection; local gameplay does not.

Banner ads occupy a separate area below the game. Interstitial ads appear between completed levels. Rewarded ads are optional: you may choose to watch one for a heart or a hint. Rewards are granted only when the advertising SDK confirms completion. Closing an ad early does not grant a reward.

Optional shop and purchases

When the shop is configured for your release, you may choose to connect your Google Play Games account to Firebase Authentication. Google processes the sign-in authorization. Firebase Authentication receives the Play Games provider identifier and display name supplied for sign-in. The app sends a Firebase authentication token to our purchase service to identify the authenticated account; it does not send your Google password. Our service verifies Google Play purchase tokens, product identifiers and purchase state using Google's developer API before crediting paid coins or ad removal.

The purchase service stores the account's Firebase user identifier, purchase receipts and tokens, item balances, ad-removal entitlement, and operation identifiers needed to prevent duplicate credit or spending. Paid coins and purchased reserve items are associated with the connected account and can be synchronized across devices. Free starter tools, weekly missions and the active heart timer remain local. These purchase records are separate from optional gameplay Analytics. Google handles payment details under its own terms; we do not receive card numbers or bank credentials.

Removal of all ads is a one-time entitlement that also hides optional rewarded ads. It does not grant unlimited hearts, tools or free replacements for ad rewards. Coin packs buy virtual in-game currency for hearts and tools. Cancellation, refund or revocation can reverse the corresponding entitlement or currency credit. Previously spent, refunded coin credit may be offset against later credits. Google Play real-time purchase notifications keep the purchase service informed of these changes.

Play Integrity security checks

From Android version 1.0.7, connected shop requests may use Google Play Integrity to help detect modified apps, unlicensed copies, unsafe devices and fraudulent requests. Local offline gameplay does not require an Integrity check. Google Play processes app metadata (package, version and signing certificate), the Play license status, device attestation information and a hash binding the check to the specific shop request. With environment verdicts enabled, Google also evaluates Play Protect status and whether other apps could capture, overlay or control the screen. The game receives risk categories, not a list of installed app names.

The authenticated LIFT purchase service sends the encrypted Integrity token to Google for verification and checks the app, license, device, request hash and token age before trusting a supplied attestation. Basic security outcomes may appear in operational logs to diagnose fraud or service failures. These security logs do not include Integrity tokens, request hashes, account identifiers or hardware attestation identifiers, and are not used to fingerprint or track people or devices. These fraud-prevention checks are separate from optional Firebase gameplay Analytics. Existing versions and temporary service outages retain the existing authenticated Google Play purchase-verification process during rollout; enabling these checks is not a promise that all cheating is prevented.

Data security

Local-only gameplay does not send progress to a developer-operated server. When you connect the shop, its authenticated service stores and checks purchase data as described above; when you choose cloud saves, Google Play Games stores private star progress; when you enable Analytics, Firebase processes the described gameplay events. Purchase requests use HTTPS and authenticated tokens. Paid balances are maintained by server transactions. The Android app blocks mixed-content loading and direct file/content access in its WebView. Local free progress is not separately encrypted by the game and depends on device security. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Your choices

Where required, Google UMP asks for advertising privacy choices before ads load. The app's hearts dialog includes an Ad privacy choices entry for Google's privacy-options form. Availability depends on Google's consent requirements. You can also manage, reset or delete your advertising identifier through supported Android settings. Watching rewarded ads is optional, and hearts also recover with time.

Delete your LIFT shop account

You can request deletion of your developer-operated LIFT shop account and associated data through our support email, without reinstalling the game. In the game, the same request page is available from Shop or Cloud save & privacy.

  1. Email ud11.support@gmail.com with the subject LIFT shop account deletion.
  2. Tell us that you want to delete your LIFT shop account and its associated data. Support will verify account ownership before acting. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or purchase tokens in your initial message.
  3. After verification, support deletes the Firebase shop account, its paid-item wallet and associated operation history. Unspent coins and reserve items may be lost. Minimum transaction records needed for refund reconciliation, fraud prevention or applicable legal obligations may need to be retained; support will explain any applicable retention.

Clearing app storage alone does not delete server records or cancel Google Play purchases. This request does not delete your Google account. Google Play Games saved progress is managed separately in Google Play Games settings; local free progress stays on your device unless you clear it.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you clear app storage or uninstall the app. Clearing storage removes saved stars, unlocks and hearts. In a browser, clearing this game's site data removes its local records. If you enabled cloud saves, clearing app storage or uninstalling does not delete the Google Play Games save. Manage or delete the game's Play Games data using Google Play Games settings. To avoid restoring old progress, also clear local game data before reconnecting. Google controls retention of advertising, Analytics and Play Games data; see its privacy policies. Disabling a feature does not by itself erase data already stored by Google. We do not sell player data or receive individual advertising identifiers through a game backend.

Permissions and personal information

The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location, and does not ask for your date of birth. Google advertising services may still infer approximate location from an IP address. If you contact support by email, we use the information you provide to answer your request. This information is handled through the email provider, not stored as a player profile inside the game.

Age eligibility and children’s privacy

LIFT City Stories is intended for adults aged 18 and over. From Android version 0.2.1, a self-declaration screen asks you to confirm that you are at least 18 before gameplay and advertising start. Only the confirmation is stored on your device; we do not collect a date of birth or identity document. This is a self-declaration, not independent identity verification. We do not intentionally request personal profiles or identifying details from children through the game. Advertising data is handled by Google's SDK as described above. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact support. This policy does not represent an independent age-verification system.

You can turn optional Analytics and cloud sync off independently in the game menu. Google Play Games account settings provide additional controls for your game profile and saved game data.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the game's data handling changes. The date above identifies the latest version.
