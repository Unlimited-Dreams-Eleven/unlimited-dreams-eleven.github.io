Window Whiskers Privacy Policy

Developer: Unlimited Dreams Eleven. Effective November 6, 2026.

Window Whiskers is a Wear OS watch face. It displays local time, date, daily step count, and watch battery level from Wear OS. The watch face uses these values on the watch and does not store or send them to the developer.

The watch face has no sign-in, advertising, analytics, or purchases inside the app. The developer does not collect or share personal data through the watch face. Google Play and Wear OS are governed by their own privacy policies.

If you email support, we use the information you send to reply to your request. You can contact us about privacy or ask us to delete your support correspondence at ud11.support@gmail.com.
