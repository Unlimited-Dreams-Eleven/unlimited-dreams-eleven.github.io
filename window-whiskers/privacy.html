Window Whiskers Privacy Policy

Developer: Unlimited Dreams Eleven. Effective November 15, 2026.

Window Whiskers displays local time, date, daily step count and watch battery level from Wear OS. These values are used on the watch and are not stored or sent to the developer.

The optional Android companion shows bundled previews and a setup guide, and saves theme and guide preferences locally. It uses Google Play services to discover connected Wear OS devices, displays a connected watch name, and uses a device identifier locally to send this app's Google Play link to the selected watch when you tap Install to wearable. The developer does not receive or retain these device names or identifiers. You complete installation in Google Play. Support email and privacy links open in another app when tapped.

The app has no sign-in, advertising, analytics or in-app purchases. Google Play, Google Play services and Wear OS are governed by their own privacy policies.

If you email support, we use the information you send to reply to your request. Contact us about privacy or request deletion of your support correspondence at ud11.support@gmail.com.
