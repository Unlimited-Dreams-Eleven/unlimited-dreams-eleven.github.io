Privacy policy for the Garmin Connect IQ edition
Effective date: October 26, 2026

Provided by Unlimited Dreams Eleven. Contact: ud11.support@gmail.com.

Scope

This policy applies to the Garmin edition, Connect IQ application ID 1ee78be68f81441abf8ae4b857a35090. The Wear OS edition has a separate policy.

Information displayed on the watch

The watch face reads local watch preferences, battery level, activity summaries, recent heart-rate history and Garmin-provided weather when available. These values are used to display the information you select. We do not pass these displayed health, activity or weather values to KiezelPay and do not operate a server that receives them. Recent heart rate may be unavailable; the watch face is not a medical device.

Settings and local storage

Appearance preferences are stored on your device. The licensing library also stores trial and license state locally. Removing the app or resetting the watch may affect local settings or license recognition; KiezelPay provides purchase restoration.

Licensing and payments

The Garmin edition uses KiezelPay for its trial and paid unlock. Its library communicates with KiezelPay to obtain purchase codes and check trial or license status, using app/device-related licensing information and normal network request information. If you purchase, you enter your email and payment information on the provider's checkout, not in this watch face. We do not receive your full payment-card details or PayPal password. Purchase and license records are processed and retained by KiezelPay and its payment providers under their own policies. See KiezelPay's privacy policy and KiezelPay's help.

Permissions

  • Communications: licensing requests through the connected phone and the platform's supported network services.
  • Background: scheduled trial and license checks.
  • Sensor History: access to recent heart-rate readings for your selected information positions.

The watch face does not request GPS location directly, record audio or access contacts. Weather is read from Garmin's weather service/cache and remains subject to Garmin's settings and policies.

Advertising and analytics

This edition does not include advertising or an analytics SDK.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email, watch model, app version, screenshot or purchase reference. We use it to investigate and reply to your request and retain it as needed for support and applicable obligations. Avoid sending passwords, full payment details or sensitive health information. Contact us to request access to or deletion of support information we hold. Requests concerning payment-provider records should also be directed to that provider.

Your choices

You can hide information positions, disable weather or motion, change settings, or uninstall the watch face. Manage device permissions through Garmin. Some licensing communications are necessary to use the paid trial/unlock service.

Website hosting

If this policy is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process website request information such as IP addresses for hosting and security. This is separate from the watch-face display data. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
