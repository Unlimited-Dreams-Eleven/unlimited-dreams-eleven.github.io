Privacy policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Corgi Dog Days (com.ud11.watchface.corgi) is a Wear OS watch face provided by Unlimited Dreams Eleven.

Data and preferences

The watch face does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer. It has no developer server, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, account login or in-app purchases. The purchase and installation are handled by Google Play.

Time, date, system weather and information from the complication providers you select are used by Wear OS to render the watch face on your device. These may include battery, steps, heart rate or other data you choose. The watch face does not send these values to us. Preferences are managed locally by the watch system.

Other services

Wear OS, your watch manufacturer, Google Play and your selected data providers operate under their own privacy policies and permissions. The watch face does not control how those services process data.

Contact and deletion

We hold no watch-face account or collected watch data to delete. Remove the face or change its selected data providers using your watch system settings. If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to include in your email and use it to respond. Do not include sensitive health information.

Contact Unlimited Dreams Eleven: ud11.support@gmail.com.
