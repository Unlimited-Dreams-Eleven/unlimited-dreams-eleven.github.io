Hanuman Thailand for Garmin

Privacy notice for Hanuman Thailand: Awakening, Garmin Connect IQ version 1.0.0 and later. Unlimited Dreams Eleven provides this watch face. Contact ud11.support@gmail.com.

Information displayed on the watch

The watch face reads the system clock, battery, activity summaries, recent heart rate and optional Garmin weather to display the data fields you select. This information stays outside license checks. Availability depends on the watch, settings and Garmin services.

Trial and licensing

The watch stores the start of your trial and, if you activate, the key and latest verification state. To verify a key it sends the key, product and app IDs and an app-specific watch identifier to Unlimited Dreams Eleven’s license service hosted on Cloudflare. The service sends the key and product ID to Gumroad to verify purchase status. The watch identifier stays with our license service. Cloudflare may process connection information to deliver and protect the service.

Cloudflare D1 stores digests of the key, sale and watch identifiers, plus binding dates, verification dates and a revision. These records are used to link one license to one watch, restore access on that watch and support replacements. Checks normally run daily. A successful verification can permit offline access for up to seven days.

Purchases and replacements

Gumroad handles checkout, payment and receipt delivery under its privacy policy. The watch face does not collect card details. For a replacement, support may request the order reference and the replacement watch’s Support ID. Each transfer records digests of the previous and replacement watch identifiers, revisions, time, operator, reason and a support case reference. License and audit records are kept while needed for licensing, support, dispute resolution or legal obligations.

Your choices

You can change displayed data or uninstall the watch face. Contact support with questions about access, correction, retention or deletion of your licensing data. Removing the binding records may affect access. Direct payment-record requests to Gumroad.
