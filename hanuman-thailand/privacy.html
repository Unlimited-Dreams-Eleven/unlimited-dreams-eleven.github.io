Privacy policy

Hanuman Thailand: Awakening — Unlimited Dreams Eleven
Effective 2 November 2026

This phone app provides offline previews and setup guidance. It has no account, ads, analytics, network permission, health access or location tracking. Preview numbers are examples.

The Wear OS watch face displays time, date and values from the complication providers you choose. These values stay within the watch/platform; we do not receive them. Provider apps have their own permissions and policies.

The optional, separate Hanuman Health watch app reads daily total calories and distance only after you enable it and grant physical activity permission. It stores the latest values locally on the watch, does not upload them, and clears them when disabled. Calories include resting and active energy.

External Google Play and email links open other apps governed by their own policies. If you email support, we receive the address and content you choose to send and use it to respond. You can request deletion of correspondence.

Uninstall the apps to remove local app data; manage complication providers in watch settings. Contact: ud11.support@gmail.com
