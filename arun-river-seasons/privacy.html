Arun River Seasons Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Unlimited Dreams Eleven develops Arun River Seasons, a Wear OS watch face and its Android phone companion (package com.ud11.arunriverseasons.watchface).

Information on your watch

The watch face displays time, date, weather and temperature supplied by Wear OS. You can select complications supplied by the watch system or other apps, such as battery, steps or heart rate. Availability depends on your watch, selected providers and their permissions. Your weather and complication providers may process location or health information under their own privacy policies. Unlimited Dreams Eleven does not receive those readings or your location.

Local settings and phone companion

Watch face preferences are stored locally by the watch system. The phone companion provides offline sample scene previews and installation guidance. Its previews contain sample data. It does not read your watch data or change watch settings. Links to Google Play and this policy open the relevant external service.

No developer data collection

These apps do not include advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, a developer server, account registration or in-app purchases. We do not collect, sell or share personal information through the apps. Google Play processes purchases and distribution under Google's policies.

Support

If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request and retain it only as needed for support or legal obligations. Do not send passwords or sensitive health information. You can request deletion of support correspondence at ud11.support@gmail.com.

This website

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting and linked services may process request information under their own policies. See GitHub's privacy statement and Google's privacy policy.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if our practices change. Questions: ud11.support@gmail.com.
