Arun River Seasons for Garmin

Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Unlimited Dreams Eleven develops this Connect IQ watch face, application ID 93f6803eb32b49889dca59111f2e762d. This policy covers the Garmin edition. The Wear OS edition has its own policy and purchase entitlement.

Information displayed locally

The face displays time, date, battery, activity counts, recent heart-rate history, distance, notifications, and weather supplied by Garmin and your watch. Available weather observation location is used on the device to calculate sunrise and sunset. We do not activate GPS or send your activity, heart rate, notifications, weather readings or weather location to our servers or KiezelPay. Missing values are shown as unavailable. Settings and cached license information are stored on your device.

Trial and purchase licensing

This paid product integrates KiezelPay for a 24-hour trial and a one-time US$2.99 unlock. Its library communicates over the internet, including background checks, to start or check a trial, show a purchase code and validate a license. These requests include an installation/account token, product identifier, device model/part number, platform and library version. The provider also receives ordinary network request information. These checks are separate from the health and weather information displayed by the face.

Checkout and restoration occur on KiezelPay's website. KiezelPay and its payment providers process the information you enter, such as your purchase email and payment details, under their policies. We do not receive your full payment-card details through the watch face. We may access transaction and license records available to us in the merchant dashboard to provide purchase support and meet applicable obligations. See KiezelPay's privacy policy and purchase and restoration help.

Permissions and optional information

SensorHistory supports the heart-rate field. Communications and Background support the KiezelPay integration. The watch face contains no advertising SDK or developer analytics service. Garmin, your phone connection and other linked services operate under their own policies. You can choose which data fields to show; a denied permission or unavailable reading may limit the corresponding field.

Support and control

Email ud11.support@gmail.com to request support or ask about information we hold. We use the correspondence you provide to respond and retain it only as needed for support or legal obligations. Do not send passwords or sensitive health information. You can remove local app settings by uninstalling the face. Uninstalling does not automatically delete transaction records held by a payment provider; direct provider data requests to that provider.
