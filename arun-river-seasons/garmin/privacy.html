Arun River Seasons for Garmin

Privacy policy for the Gumroad-compatible edition

Unlimited Dreams Eleven develops this Garmin Connect IQ watch face. This policy applies to Garmin version 1.2.0 and later. The Wear OS edition uses a separate purchase and privacy policy.

If your watch is running Garmin version 1.0.1 or earlier, see the privacy policy for the earlier Garmin version.

Information displayed on the watch

The watch face reads time, battery, activity, recent heart-rate history, distance, notification count and Garmin weather. It uses the weather observation location on the watch for sunrise and sunset. The license service does not receive these readings or the weather location.

Checkout and activation

Gumroad handles checkout, payment and order delivery under its own privacy policy. The watch face does not receive full payment card details. To activate a license and recheck it, the face sends the license key, product and app identifiers, and a Garmin app-scoped device identifier to our Cloudflare-hosted license service over HTTPS via Garmin Connect. The same identifier is displayed as the Support ID. The service checks the purchase with Gumroad without increasing Gumroad's Uses count.

The watch stores the verified key, purchase identity, binding and last verification time so it can recheck the license and provide up to seven days of offline access after its last successful check. The service stores hashed binding identifiers, verification times and support transfer history, including a case reference, reason and revision. It does not send order email, payment details or the order number to the watch. The service and payment provider may process standard network request information. Existing purchases and support

On update, the watch can read its previous locally verified Arun purchase record to preserve access. An uninstall or device reset can remove local data. If you need help with an earlier purchase or moving to a replacement watch, contact ud11.support@gmail.com with order details and the Support ID. We use those details to verify the request and keep records as necessary to manage the license, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations. Keep the key private and do not send passwords, card details or health data.

Permissions

Garmin SensorHistory supports the heart-rate field. Communications and Background Activity support online license checks. The face does not include advertising or an analytics SDK.
