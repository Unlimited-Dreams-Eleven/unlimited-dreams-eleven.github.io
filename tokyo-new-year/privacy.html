Tokyo New Year — Privacy Policy

Unlimited Dreams Eleven
Effective date: November 3, 2026

Tokyo New Year (com.ud11.tokyonewyear.watchface) is a Wear OS watch face provided by Unlimited Dreams Eleven.

Data used on the watch

The watch face displays time, date, battery level, steps, heart rate, weather temperature and information from complication providers you choose. Wear OS, your watch manufacturer and selected apps supply these values under their own permissions and policies. The face uses them to draw its display locally and does not transmit them to Unlimited Dreams Eleven.

No developer data collection or tracking

The watch face is a resource-only Watch Face Format package. It has no developer-operated server, account login, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, network client or in-app purchasing system. It does not collect or share your displayed health, location or usage values with the developer. A paid download, if offered, is processed by Google Play under Google's own policies.

Settings and choices

Style and complication preferences are managed locally by the watch system. You can change data providers, turn animation off or uninstall the face. The operating system controls local preferences and their removal. The developer holds no watch-face usage profile on a server to delete. Heart rate and activity information are for display only, not diagnosis or medical advice.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and information you choose to provide. We use them to answer and resolve your request, retaining correspondence only as needed for support or applicable requirements. You may request deletion by contacting us. Please do not send unnecessary sensitive information.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy if the product or its data practices change and will update the effective date on this page.
