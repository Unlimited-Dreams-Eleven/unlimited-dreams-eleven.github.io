Privacy Policy

Unlimited Dreams Eleven · Effective 18 November 2026

Information used by the app

The NIMIT ARCANA Android closed-testing build generates sample readings on the device. It has no app accounts, ads, analytics, real-money purchases, or network-based AI. The developer does not receive or share the information you enter in the app.

Data on your device

The app uses your name or nickname, birth date, typed questions, and birth time and city where needed. These entries are held in memory during use and are not saved in your history. The app stores test coins, sample reading history, reading topics, and follow-up progress on the device. Readings may include a zodiac sign or a numerology result derived from your birth date.

Retention and deletion

Data on your device remains until you clear app storage or uninstall the app. To remove it, open Android Settings → Apps → NIMIT ARCANA → Storage → Clear storage. The developer cannot access the data that the app stores on your device.

Support and external services

If you contact support, we use the email address and message you send to handle and respond to your request. We retain correspondence as needed to resolve the request. For privacy questions or to request deletion of your correspondence, contact ud11.support@gmail.com. Google Play downloads and test enrollment are subject to Google’s privacy policy.
