Privacy Policy

Unlimited Dreams Eleven · For test builds

Information used by the app

NIMIT ARCANA for Android generates sample readings and scores quizzes on your device. Players can use it without an account. The test build uses simulated coins and does not call a network-based AI service.

Data on your device

Starting with version 0.1.11, saving a profile stores its name or nickname and date of birth on the device so you can use it again or keep several profiles. Profile information is not sent to a server. Typed questions, birth times, and birth cities are held in memory during use. The app stores a test coin balance shared across profiles, along with sample reading history, reading topics, and follow-up progress on the device. Readings may include a zodiac sign or a numerology result derived from a birth date.

Quiz answers, results and unlock status stay in memory for the current round. If you choose to share, the app briefly keeps a card containing the quiz title, result name and summary to send to the app you choose.

Test ads

In the ad-enabled build, choosing to watch an ad uses Google's consent tools and requests a test ad from Google Mobile Ads. Google's SDK may collect and share IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, interactions, diagnostics and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google encrypts this traffic in transit. The build removes the Android advertising ID permission. App ad requests do not include your name, birth date, typed questions or quiz answers. See the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

This test build includes its quiz catalogue on the device. It does not connect to Firestore.

Retention and deletion

Data on your device remains until you clear app storage or uninstall the app. To remove it, open Android Settings → Apps → NIMIT ARCANA → Storage → Clear storage. The developer cannot access the data that the app stores on your device.

Support and external services

If you contact support, we use the email address and message you send to handle and respond to your request. We retain correspondence as needed to resolve the request. For privacy questions or to request deletion of your correspondence, contact ud11.support@gmail.com. Google Play downloads and test enrollment are subject to Google’s privacy policy.
