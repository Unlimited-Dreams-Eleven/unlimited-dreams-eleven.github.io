Privacy Policy

Little Lost is provided by Unlimited Dreams Eleven. Updated: 21 October 2026.

Game progress

Stars, unlocked scenes, completed scenes, language, best times, daily star-ad quota, audio settings and policy acknowledgement are stored locally. There is no player account or game server. Android device backups may include app data depending on device settings.

Advertising

The Android app uses Google AdMob. Google Mobile Ads may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Non-personalized ads can still process data. Development builds use test ads.

Data security

Game progress is kept in the app’s local storage rather than sent to a game server. It is not separately encrypted by the game; access also depends on device security and backup settings. The Android app disallows mixed-content loading in its WebView. Advertising data is handled by Google’s SDK and services under their security practices. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your device and app updated and protect access to your device.

Your choices

Where required, Google UMP asks for advertising privacy choices before ads load. Ad privacy choices are available in the scene picker when required by Google. You may also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you clear app storage or uninstall the app, subject to device backup settings. Clearing storage removes stars and unlocks. Google controls retention of data handled by its advertising services; see its privacy policy. We do not sell player data or receive copies of individual advertising identifiers through a game backend.

Children

We do not ask players to enter names, dates of birth or contact information. Parents and guardians can contact us using the email below with questions about a child’s privacy.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Unlimited Dreams Eleven at ud11.support@gmail.com.
