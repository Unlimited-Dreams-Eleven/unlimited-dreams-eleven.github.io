FitSpectrum Garmin Privacy

Provided by Unlimited Dreams Eleven for the Garmin edition of FitSpectrum.

Watch data

Time, battery, fitness readings and cached weather come from Garmin on the watch. FitSpectrum uses them locally for display. Garmin controls data availability and permissions. The license service does not receive those readings.

Purchases and activation

Gumroad processes orders, payments and receipts under its own privacy policy. To verify a key and bind it to a single watch, FitSpectrum sends the key, product and app identifiers, and an app-scoped Garmin device identifier over HTTPS to the owner's Cloudflare-hosted license service. The service verifies the key with Gumroad. Checkout card information is not sent to the watch or stored by the license service.

The binding database stores scoped digests of keys, sale identifiers and device identifiers, along with verification times, revision and support history. The watch stores its activation record locally to allow verification and a limited offline period. Network providers may process connection metadata to deliver and secure the service.

Support and your choices

For a license transfer we verify the original order and use the replacement watch's Support ID. Transfer records help resolve activation issues and prevent unauthorized changes. Contact ud11.support@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion. Deleting a binding can affect access to the purchased license. Records needed for legitimate order, fraud prevention and legal obligations may be retained.

See the Gumroad Privacy Policy and Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
