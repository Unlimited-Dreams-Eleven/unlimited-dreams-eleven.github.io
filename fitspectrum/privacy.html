FitSpectrum — Privacy Policy

Provided by Unlimited Dreams Eleven. Updated 1 November 2026. Android package: com.ud11.fitspectrum.watchface.

Watch face and local information

FitSpectrum is a standalone Wear OS watch face using Watch Face Format. It displays time, date, battery level and data from complication providers you select, such as steps, heart rate, calories or weather. Selected providers and the operating system control permissions, measurement, data availability and update timing. The watch face does not independently measure heart rate, track workouts or obtain your location. Missing provider data is shown as unavailable.

Collection and sharing

We do not collect, transmit, store on our servers, sell or share personal or health data through this Wear OS watch face. It includes no developer backend, analytics, advertising SDKs, account system or network permissions. Theme and complication choices are handled locally by Wear OS. Data is used for display on your device.

Phone companion

The optional FitSpectrum phone app provides bundled static watch face previews and installation help. It does not connect to the watch or read fitness, heart-rate, location or complication data. It has no account, advertising, analytics, backend, network client or requested permissions. A selected preview is retained locally while the app is recreated. Buttons open Google Play, this privacy page or your email app using system actions; those services follow their own policies, and no support email is sent automatically.

Other services

Google Play handles purchases, distribution and testing under its own policies. Wear OS and chosen complication providers may process information under their respective policies and permissions. FitSpectrum does not receive your payment information. If you contact support, we receive the information you voluntarily send and use it to respond to your request.

Your choices and deletion

You can change or remove complication providers in the watch face editor, manage their permissions in device settings, and uninstall the watch face. Provider-held data is managed through the provider. Contact support to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to records we must retain.

Health information

Displayed fitness information is for personal reference. FitSpectrum does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment or medical monitoring.
