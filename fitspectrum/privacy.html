FitSpectrum — Privacy Policy

Provided by Unlimited Dreams Eleven. Updated 17 November 2026. Android package: com.ud11.fitspectrum.watchface.

Watch face and local information

FitSpectrum is a standalone Wear OS watch face using Watch Face Format. It displays time, date, battery level and data from complication providers you select, such as steps, heart rate, calories or weather. Selected providers and the operating system control permissions, measurement, data availability and update timing. The watch face does not independently measure heart rate, track workouts or obtain your location. Missing provider data is shown as unavailable.

Collection and sharing

We do not collect, transmit, store on our servers, sell or share personal or health data through this Wear OS watch face. It includes no developer backend, analytics, advertising SDKs, account system or network permissions. Theme and complication choices are handled locally by Wear OS. Data is used for display on your device.

Phone companion

The optional FitSpectrum phone app provides bundled static previews, installation help and locally saved preview, onboarding and appearance preferences. Android backup is disabled. It does not read fitness, heart-rate, location or complication data and has no developer account, advertising, analytics or developer backend.

When you choose Install to wearable or refresh connected watches, Google Play services discovers connected Wear OS watches. Their display names and transient node IDs are held in Activity memory to select the destination; we do not log them, save them or send them to our servers. AndroidX RemoteActivityHelper uses the Wear connection to open the public FitSpectrum Google Play listing on the selected watch. If discovery or remote launch fails, the app offers Google Play on your phone. Opening a listing does not install or activate the watch face: you complete installation and any purchase in Google Play.

The companion requests no health, location or Bluetooth runtime permissions. AndroidX includes an app-owned signature permission protecting internal dynamic receivers; it grants no access to those data. Google Play services and Google Play process information under Google's policies. Other buttons open sharing, Google Play, this privacy page or your email app using system actions; no support email is sent automatically. Removing the app removes its local preferences according to Android behavior.

Other services

Google Play handles purchases, distribution and testing under its own policies. Wear OS and chosen complication providers may process information under their respective policies and permissions. FitSpectrum does not receive your payment information. If you contact support, we receive the information you voluntarily send and use it to respond to your request.

Garmin edition

The separate Garmin edition has different on-watch data and licensing behavior. See the dedicated FitSpectrum Garmin Privacy Policy for Gumroad payment, license verification and retention details.

Your choices and deletion

You can change or remove complication providers in the watch face editor, manage their permissions in device settings, and uninstall the watch face. Provider-held data is managed through the provider. Contact support to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to records we must retain.

Health information

Displayed fitness information is for personal reference. FitSpectrum does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment or medical monitoring.
